Professional Profile

I am a highly accomplished malaria and HIV/AIDS researcher and clinical pharmacist specializing in cost-effectiveness analysis, pharmacoepidemiology, and health systems evaluation. My core focus centers on engineering high-impact, scalable interventions to dramatically reduce infectious disease burdens through economic, clinical, and humanistic outcome mapping.

By blending raw field implementation experience via the Ghana Health Service with sophisticated quantitative modeling tools like R, TreeAge, and Stata, I build actionable frameworks to inform critical resource allocation, policy formation, and health systems optimization in low-resource environments.

Advanced Analytical Work

Cost-effectiveness modeling combining RTS,S & SMC strategies, alongside comprehensive ECHO-model HIV therapy evaluations.
